Question:-What are JavaScript promise utility functions
Answer:-
Promise.all():-Run Multiple Promise in parallel resolve when all resolve.Reject immediately if any rejects.
Promise.allSettled:-Waits for all promises, whether success or failure.
Promise.race():-Return result of first completed promise.(success/failure)
promise.any():-Return first successful promise.
Promise.resolve():-immediately resolve
Promise.reject():-immediately reject.
